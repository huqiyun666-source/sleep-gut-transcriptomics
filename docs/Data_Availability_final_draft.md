# Data Availability statement — final draft

## A. BEFORE_REPOSITORY_PUBLICATION

The public datasets analysed in this study are available under GSE289089 / PRJNA1221288, PRJNA1167170, and PRJNA862187. A curated code and processed-results package is available at https://github.com/huqiyun666-source/sleep-gut-transcriptomics. The Zenodo DOI is pending / TO_BE_ASSIGNED; raw sequencing reads, reference resources, and large intermediate objects are not redistributed in the package.

## B. AFTER_ZENODO_DOI

The public datasets analysed in this study are available under GSE289089 / PRJNA1221288, PRJNA1167170, and PRJNA862187. Analysis code, manuscript-critical processed tables, and provenance records are available at https://github.com/huqiyun666-source/sleep-gut-transcriptomics and will be archived at Zenodo under `[ZENODO_DOI]` after assignment. Raw sequencing reads remain available from the originating public archives and are not redistributed in the repository.
